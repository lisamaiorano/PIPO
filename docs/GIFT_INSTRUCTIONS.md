# Gift Instructions for Lisa

This file is for you.

## What To Prepare

1. Publish the site or put it on a phone/laptop where Pipo can open it.
2. Generate a QR code that points to the final game URL.
3. Print the QR code.
4. Optional: print or 3D-print the access card.
5. Put everything in a small envelope or box.

Suggested outside text:

```text
CLASSIFIED

FOR: PIPO

DO NOT OPEN
UNLESS YOU ARE READY TO DEBUG THE UNIVERSE.
```

Alternative:

```text
MISSION BRIEFING

TARGET: PIPO
CLEARANCE: CLASSIFIED
DATE: 14.04

SCAN TO BEGIN.
```

## Delivery

Give him the envelope and let him scan the QR.

Try not to explain too much at the beginning. The best effect is:

```text
"Che cazzo è questa cosa?"
```

Then:

```text
"Ok, questa è fatta bene."
```

Then:

```text
"Ah, aspetta..."
```

## If He Gets Stuck

Tell him to press `Hint`.

If he still gets stuck, you can give a tiny nudge:

- Star map: "Look at the x coordinate."
- Decoder: "Two steps, not one."
- Log: "The fragments are day, month, year."
- Logic: "Make a truth table."
- Terminal: "Try `help`, then `ls`."
- Beach: "Do not improve history."

## What Happens At The End

The final reveal says the signal is not from a star, satellite, or alien source. It is from Lisa.

The message references:

- 14 April 2025
- the beach
- your confession
- his answer, exactly: `"chill"`
- the fact that this became the beginning of everything

## QR Placement

If you use the 3D card, put the QR code on the back. A printed sticker is easiest and more reliable than printing raised QR geometry.

Recommended sticker size:

```text
28 mm × 28 mm or larger
```

Test the sticker from normal phone distance before gifting.
